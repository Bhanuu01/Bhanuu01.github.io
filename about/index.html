About

About Bhanuja Karumuru

I am an M.S. Computer Engineering student at NYU Tandon, with a background in electronics and signal processing from NIT Sikkim and professional backend engineering experience from SalesUp.

Also searchable as Bhanu Karumuru, B. Karumuru, and BK3170.

Where I started

NIT Sikkim gave me my first serious technical foundation

I studied electronics and communication engineering at NIT Sikkim, where a lot of my early work was in signal processing, communication systems, and applied machine learning. Coming from a smaller institute taught me to be resourceful and to learn by building, not just by following a predefined path.

Industry

SalesUp is where I learned what production work actually feels like

At SalesUp I worked on backend APIs, async pipelines, and LLM-backed product features. The most useful lesson from that year was learning how much better a system gets when responsibilities move off the request path and into a cleaner background workflow.

Why NYU

I came to NYU to go deeper on systems work

NYU gave me room to move closer to the kinds of problems I want to keep working on: CUDA performance, ML systems, robotics perception, and secure systems. I am building toward roles where I can work on infrastructure and engineering problems that stay meaningful after the first demo.

A more personal note

I naturally turn technical work into explanations

One pattern that keeps showing up in my work is that I like making complicated things easier to follow, whether that means writing clearer feedback for students, turning a rough project into a readable case study, or building systems with output that someone else can actually use.

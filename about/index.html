Current stage

From NYU to an incoming Amazon internship

Right now I am building depth in systems work at NYU while preparing to join Amazon as an incoming Software Development Engineer Intern. I am especially interested in work that combines measurement, reliability, and real operational constraints.

Background

Signal processing first, systems work after

My technical foundation started in electronics, communication, and signal processing at NIT Sikkim. That led to research in dysarthria severity classification, then to backend engineering at SalesUp, and now to a broader interest in ML systems and infrastructure.

What I like working on

Projects with a measurable systems question behind them

The common thread across the site is fairly simple: I like work where the behavior can be measured, the tradeoffs can be explained, and the result stays useful after the first prototype. That can mean a faster backend path, a more reviewable ML pipeline, or a lower-level CUDA experiment.

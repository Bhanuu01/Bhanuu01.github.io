Flagship case study

Fused Linear Attention

A CUDA project built around a systems question rather than a benchmark alone: how much overhead can be removed from transformer inference when projection and attention are fused into a tighter execution path?

The work focused on shared-memory layout, tiling strategy, and profiler-guided reasoning about where HBM traffic was actually being spent. That made it less of a pure optimization exercise and more of a careful systems study of what the kernel was doing on H100 hardware.

11–55% HBM bandwidth reduction on H100, with correctness validated across all test cases.
1.22x–1.41x speedup 85–91% lower peak allocation
